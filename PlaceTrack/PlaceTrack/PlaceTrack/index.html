Why PlaceTrack exists

Most students track placements across five different places — a notes app for applications, WhatsApp for drive updates, a notebook for prep, and memory for deadlines. PlaceTrack pulls all of it into a single dashboard built specifically for the placement cycle, from the first application to the final offer letter.

This is a Phase 1 frontend prototype — every screen you see here is fully built and interactive, running entirely in your browser with sample data.
